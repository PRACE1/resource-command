    SAR Cross-Check — Declared vs Satellite-Measured Output
    Sentinel-1 SAR (C-band radar, 10m resolution, 12-day revisit cycle) from ESA Copernicus measures tailings pile volume change using DEM differencing. Measurement accuracy is ±8–12%. The 5% MRCA threshold is deliberately set below this uncertainty band — so only statistically significant variances trigger audits. The satellite does not convict. It triggers. The MRC/ZRA audit is the investigation.

    Blockchain Architecture — Technical Specification
    Every compliance event, audit trigger, and royalty verification is immutably recorded on a permissioned three-node SHA-256 blockchain. The platform vendor holds no node — custody is designed for distribution across government institutions.
    Node 1 — MRC (Designated)
    Minerals Regulation Commission. Designed as the primary regulatory node. Receives all production and compliance data first.
    Online
    Node 2 — ZESCO (Designated)
    Zambia Electricity Supply Corporation. Designed as the energy data correlation node. Cross-validates mine energy consumption against declared output.
    Online
    Node 3 — Auditor General (Designated)
    Independent oversight. Designed so the AGO node ensures no single institution can alter the record. Required for BFT consensus.
    Online
      Consensus rule: Two of three nodes must agree before any block is confirmed. Tampering with any historical record breaks every subsequent hash in the chain immediately — making silent alteration mathematically impossible. The platform vendor holds no node and cannot write to or alter the chain.
