# CONSTITUTION
## Kgosi Sovereign Holdings Proprietary Limited
**Private Company Limited by Shares**
Incorporated under the Companies Act of Botswana (Cap 42:01)

---

## PART 1 — PRELIMINARY

### 1. Interpretation

In this Constitution, unless the context otherwise requires:

- **"Act"** means the Companies Act of Botswana, Cap 42:01, and any statutory modification or re-enactment thereof for the time being in force.
- **"Board"** means the board of directors of the Company for the time being.
- **"Company"** means Kgosi Sovereign Holdings Proprietary Limited.
- **"Constitution"** means this document, as amended from time to time by Special Resolution.
- **"Director"** means a person appointed to the Board in accordance with this Constitution.
- **"Founder"** means the initial sole subscriber to this Constitution and first Director, Kennedy Oagile Morapedi, or their lawfully appointed successor in title.
- **"IP Assets"** means all intellectual property owned or licensed by the Company, including but not limited to: source code, ZK circuit designs, cryptographic protocol specifications, system architecture documentation, trade secrets, patents, trademarks, and software licences relating to the Resource Command platform and any successor or derivative works.
- **"Ordinary Resolution"** means a resolution passed by a simple majority (more than 50%) of the votes of shareholders entitled to vote and voting on the resolution.
- **"Register"** means the register of members of the Company maintained under the Act.
- **"SAFE"** means a Simple Agreement for Future Equity in the form approved by the Board.
- **"Share"** means an ordinary share in the capital of the Company unless otherwise specified.
- **"Shareholder"** means any person registered in the Register as the holder of one or more Shares.
- **"Special Resolution"** means a resolution passed by not less than 75% of the votes of shareholders entitled to vote and voting.
- **"Subsidiary"** means any company in which the Company holds a controlling interest, including Kgosi Sovereign Holdings Zambia Limited and any future operating entities.
- **"Transfer"** means any sale, assignment, pledge, encumbrance, gift, or other disposition of Shares, whether voluntary or involuntary.
- **"Written"** includes electronic communication where both parties have agreed to that mode in writing.

Words importing the singular include the plural and vice versa. Headings are for reference only and do not affect interpretation.

## PART 2 — COMPANY IDENTITY

### 2. Name

The name of the Company is **Kgosi Sovereign Holdings Proprietary Limited**.

### 3. Registered Office

The registered office of the Company shall be situated in Botswana at such address as the Board may from time to time determine and notify to CIPA.

### 4. Objects and Purpose

4.1 The Company is incorporated as a **technology holding company** with the following primary objects:

  (a) To own, protect, develop, license, and exploit all IP Assets relating to the Resource Command platform, a zero-knowledge proof-based sovereign natural resource royalty verification and data integrity system;

  (b) To hold equity interests in Subsidiary operating companies established to deploy and commercialise the Resource Command platform, with initial focus on Zambia and the broader African continent;

  (c) To receive, manage, and deploy grant funding, development finance, and private investment capital in furtherance of the above objects;

  (d) To enter into licensing agreements, joint venture arrangements, and commercial contracts in furtherance of the above objects;

  (e) To do all such other things as are incidental or conducive to the attainment of the above objects.

4.2 The Company shall not carry on any business that requires a licence, permit, or statutory approval without first obtaining that approval.

### 5. Liability

The liability of each Shareholder is limited to the amount (if any) unpaid on the Shares held by that Shareholder.

## PART 3 — SHARE CAPITAL

### 6. Authorised Share Capital

6.1 The authorised share capital of the Company is **100,000 ordinary shares** of no par value.

6.2 The Board may, by Ordinary Resolution of Shareholders, increase the authorised share capital at any time.

### 7. Initial Issued Shares

7.1 At incorporation, **65,000 ordinary shares** (65%) are issued and allotted to the Founder, fully paid.

7.2 The remaining **35,000 shares** (35%) are reserved as follows:

  | Pool | Shares | % | Purpose |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Investor Pool | 25,000 | 25% | External equity investors (including SAFE conversions) |
  | Strategic Partner Pool | 5,000 | 5% | Government co-investors, multilateral development agencies |
  | Employee & Advisor Pool | 5,000 | 5% | Key hires, technical advisors, and board advisors |

7.3 No shares in the reserved pools shall be allotted without a resolution of the Board specifying the allottee, the number of shares, the price or consideration, and the vesting conditions (if any).

### 8. SAFE Instruments

8.1 The Board may issue SAFE instruments to investors without constituting an allotment of Shares. SAFEs shall convert to Shares upon a Qualified Financing Event (as defined in the applicable SAFE agreement) on the terms set out in the SAFE.

8.2 The aggregate principal amount of all outstanding SAFEs shall not, without a Special Resolution, exceed **USD 500,000** during the pilot and pre-seed stages.

8.3 SAFE holders have no voting rights, no right to dividends, and no right to any information except as expressly provided in their SAFE agreement, until and unless their SAFE converts to Shares.

### 9. Classes of Shares

9.1 All Shares issued under this Constitution are ordinary shares carrying equal rights, unless and until the Board creates a separate class of preference shares by Special Resolution.

9.2 Preference shares may only be created if approved by a Special Resolution. Any preference share terms must not dilute the Founder's voting control below 51% of total voting rights without the Founder's written consent.

## PART 4 — TRANSFER OF SHARES

### 10. Restrictions on Transfer

10.1 **No Shareholder may Transfer any Share** without first complying with the right of first refusal procedure in clause 11.

10.2 Any purported Transfer in breach of this clause 10 is **void** and shall not be registered in the Register.

10.3 Shares may not be pledged, mortgaged, or otherwise encumbered as security without prior written Board approval, which shall not be unreasonably withheld in the case of institutional lending against the Founder's Shares.

### 11. Right of First Refusal (ROFR)

11.1 A Shareholder wishing to Transfer Shares (the **"Selling Shareholder"**) must give written notice to the Company (the **"Transfer Notice"**) specifying:
  (a) The number of Shares to be Transferred;
  (b) The proposed price per Share;
  (c) The identity of the proposed transferee.

11.2 The Company shall within **10 business days** of receiving the Transfer Notice notify all other Shareholders of the proposed Transfer. Each Shareholder (other than the Selling Shareholder) has the right to purchase their pro-rata share of the offered Shares at the proposed price within **20 business days** of that notice.

11.3 If the existing Shareholders do not take up all the offered Shares within the 20-day period, the unsold balance may be transferred to the proposed transferee at no less than the proposed price, within **60 days** of the original Transfer Notice.

11.4 If the Transfer to the proposed transferee is not completed within 60 days, the Transfer Notice lapses and the procedure must recommence.

### 12. Compulsory Transfer — Bad Leaver

12.1 A Shareholder (other than the Founder) who:
  (a) is convicted of a criminal offence involving fraud or dishonesty;
  (b) is declared insolvent;
  (c) materially breaches this Constitution and fails to remedy the breach within 30 days of written notice; or
  (d) in the case of an employee-Shareholder, whose employment or engagement with the Company is terminated for cause,

  shall be deemed a **"Bad Leaver"** and must offer all their Shares for sale to the Company at the lower of (i) the original subscription price and (ii) the fair market value determined by the Company's auditors.

12.2 The Founder is exempt from the Bad Leaver provisions.

### 13. Permitted Transfers

13.1 The following Transfers are permitted without triggering clause 11, subject to prior written Board approval (not to be unreasonably withheld):

  (a) A Transfer by the Founder to a wholly-owned holding company or trust for estate planning purposes, provided the Founder retains ultimate beneficial ownership and voting control;
  (b) A Transfer to a spouse or lineal descendant of the Founder by way of gift, subject to the transferee executing a Deed of Adherence to this Constitution.

## PART 5 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

### 14. Ownership of IP Assets

14.1 All IP Assets developed by or for the Company — including but not limited to the Resource Command zero-knowledge royalty verification system, the tamper-proof IoT transit tracking integration, satellite revenue corroboration algorithms, and all related cryptographic protocols — are and shall remain the **exclusive property of the Company**.

14.2 No Director, employee, contractor, advisor, or Shareholder may claim any personal ownership of, or licence to, any IP Asset without a separate written IP assignment agreement approved by the Board.

### 15. IP Assignment Obligation

15.1 Every Director, employee, and contractor engaged by the Company shall, as a condition of engagement, execute an **IP Assignment and Confidentiality Agreement** assigning to the Company all IP created in the course of their work.

15.2 Failure to execute such an agreement renders the engagement voidable at the Company's option.

### 16. Licensing to Subsidiaries

16.1 The Company may license IP Assets to its Subsidiaries on arm's-length commercial terms, subject to a written intercompany licensing agreement approved by the Board.

16.2 No IP Asset shall be permanently transferred (as opposed to licensed) to any Subsidiary without a Special Resolution of Shareholders.

16.3 No IP Asset shall be licensed or transferred to any third party outside the group on terms that grant exclusivity, without a Special Resolution.

## PART 6 — DIRECTORS

### 17. Number of Directors

17.1 The Company shall have a minimum of **one (1)** and a maximum of **five (5)** Directors.

17.2 The Founder shall be a Director for so long as they hold Shares in the Company. The Founder may not be removed as a Director by any other Shareholder while holding any Shares.

### 18. Appointment and Removal of Directors

18.1 The Founder may, by written notice to the Company, appoint or remove any Director at any time.

18.2 Additional Directors (beyond those appointed by the Founder) may be appointed by Ordinary Resolution of Shareholders.

18.3 A Director (other than the Founder) may be removed by Ordinary Resolution of Shareholders, or by written notice from the Founder.

18.4 A Director shall cease to hold office if they:
  (a) resign by written notice;
  (b) become mentally incapacitated;
  (c) are declared insolvent or bankrupt;
  (d) are convicted of a criminal offence involving dishonesty;
  (e) are absent without leave from three consecutive Board meetings without cause.

### 19. Powers of the Board

19.1 Subject to this Constitution and the Act, the Board has the authority to manage and direct all business and affairs of the Company.

19.2 The following actions **require a Board Resolution** (i.e., may not be taken by any single Director acting alone):

  (a) Allotment or issue of new Shares or SAFE instruments;
  (b) Execution of any contract with a value exceeding **USD 10,000**;
  (c) Entering into any financing arrangement (loan, overdraft, guarantee);
  (d) Appointment of auditors, attorneys, or key service providers;
  (e) Opening or closing of any bank account;
  (f) Any acquisition or disposal of assets valued above **USD 5,000**;
  (g) Commencement or settlement of any litigation;
  (h) Establishment of, or material change to, any Subsidiary;
  (i) Approval of intercompany licensing agreements with Subsidiaries.

19.3 The following actions **require a Special Resolution of Shareholders**:

  (a) Amendment of this Constitution;
  (b) Permanent transfer of any IP Asset outside the group;
  (c) Creation of a new class of shares;
  (d) Merger, acquisition, or disposal of the Company or any material part of its business;
  (e) Voluntary winding-up;
  (f) Any transaction that would dilute the Founder's voting rights below 51%;
  (g) Increase of outstanding SAFE principal beyond USD 500,000.

### 20. Remuneration of Directors

20.1 Directors shall not be entitled to remuneration for their services as Directors unless approved by Ordinary Resolution.

20.2 The Founder, in their capacity as executive, may receive a salary and benefits as determined by the Board from time to time.

20.3 Directors shall be reimbursed for all reasonable expenses incurred in connection with the business of the Company, subject to submission of receipts.

### 21. Quorum for Board Meetings

21.1 The quorum for a Board meeting is a **majority of the Directors** in office at the time.

21.2 While the Company has only one Director (the Founder), all powers exercisable by the Board may be exercised by the Founder alone.

21.3 Board meetings may be held in person, by telephone, or by video conference. A Director participating by remote means is deemed present for quorum purposes.

## PART 7 — CONFLICT OF INTEREST

### 22. Declaration of Interest

22.1 A Director who has, directly or indirectly, an interest in a transaction or matter before the Board must **declare that interest** at the earliest opportunity, and the declaration must be recorded in the minutes.

22.2 An interested Director shall not vote on any resolution in which they have a direct financial interest, unless all other Directors consent in writing.

### 23. Investor-Director Dual Role

23.1 Where any Shareholder also acts as legal counsel, financial advisor, or technical advisor to the Company, they must execute a **written Conflict of Interest Waiver** acknowledging their dual role before rendering any advice to the Company.

23.2 Any legal or advisory fees paid to a Shareholder who is also a Director or advisor must be on arm's-length terms documented in a separate written engagement agreement, approved by the Board without the participation of the interested Shareholder.

### 24. Confidentiality

24.1 Every Director, Shareholder, and officer of the Company must keep confidential all non-public information regarding the Company's business, technology, clients, partners, and financial affairs, and must not disclose such information to any third party without prior written Board approval.

24.2 This obligation survives resignation, removal, or disposal of Shares, for a period of **five (5) years** from the date of departure.

## PART 8 — SHAREHOLDERS

### 25. Shareholders' Meetings

25.1 The Company shall hold an Annual General Meeting (AGM) within six months of the end of each financial year, or as required by the Act.

25.2 The Board may call an Extraordinary General Meeting at any time. Shareholders holding not less than 10% of the total issued Shares may requisition a meeting in writing.

25.3 Notice of at least **14 days** (21 days for Special Resolutions) must be given to all Shareholders.

25.4 Meetings may be held in person, by telephone, or by video conference.

### 26. Voting Rights

26.1 Each ordinary Share carries **one vote** on a poll.

26.2 On a show of hands, each Shareholder present (in person or by proxy) has one vote.

26.3 The Founder's Shares carry an additional **weighted voting right** such that, in respect of any matter that could reduce the Founder's control of the Company (as listed in clause 19.3), the Founder's vote counts as **three votes per Share** held by the Founder. This weighted voting right is personal to the Founder and does not transfer to any other holder of the Founder's Shares.

26.4 A Shareholder may vote by proxy. The proxy form must be submitted to the Company at least 48 hours before the meeting.

### 27. Written Resolutions

27.1 Any resolution that could be passed at a meeting of Shareholders may instead be passed as a written resolution signed (or consented to electronically) by Shareholders holding the requisite majority, without holding a meeting.

## PART 9 — ACCOUNTS AND DIVIDENDS

### 28. Financial Year

The financial year of the Company shall end on **31 December** each year, unless otherwise determined by the Board.

### 29. Accounts and Audit

29.1 The Board shall cause proper accounting records to be kept and financial statements to be prepared in accordance with International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) or such other standards as required by law or grant covenants.

29.2 The Company shall appoint an auditor annually at the AGM if required by the Act or any grant agreement.

29.3 Grant funds must be maintained in **a separate designated bank account** and reconciled separately from commercial operational accounts. Grant expenditure must be recorded against the approved budget lines in the relevant grant agreement.

### 30. Dividends

30.1 The Board may recommend the payment of dividends, subject to approval by Ordinary Resolution.

30.2 **No dividends shall be declared or paid** while:
  (a) any grant disbursement agreement contains a restriction on profit distributions; or
  (b) the Company is unable to pay its debts as they fall due; or
  (c) the Zambian Subsidiary has outstanding shareholder loans owed to the Company that have not been repaid.

30.3 Dividends shall be paid pro-rata to Shareholders according to their shareholding at the time of the dividend declaration.

---

## PART 10 — WINDING UP

### 31. Voluntary Winding Up

31.1 The Company may be wound up voluntarily by Special Resolution.

31.2 In the event of winding up, the assets of the Company shall be applied in the following order:
  (a) Payment of the Company's debts and liabilities (including any outstanding grant repayment obligations);
  (b) Payment of the costs of winding up;
  (c) Distribution of the surplus to Shareholders **pro-rata to their shareholdings**.

### 32. IP Assets on Winding Up

32.1 In the event of winding up, the Founder shall have a **right of first offer** to acquire the IP Assets at fair market value (as determined by an independent expert) before those assets are offered to any third party.

32.2 IP Assets may not be transferred to any entity that competes directly with the stated mission of enabling sovereign natural resource revenue transparency in African jurisdictions, without a Special Resolution of the then-current Shareholders.

---

## PART 11 — GENERAL

### 33. Notices

33.1 Any notice required under this Constitution may be given in writing by hand delivery, registered post, or electronic mail to the address or email address of the recipient as recorded in the Register.

33.2 A notice sent by email is deemed received 24 hours after dispatch, unless the sender receives a delivery failure notification.

### 34. Amendment of Constitution

34.1 This Constitution may only be amended by **Special Resolution** (75% of votes cast).

34.2 No amendment shall be made that:
  (a) removes or reduces the Founder's weighted voting rights under clause 26.3 without the Founder's written consent;
  (b) removes the IP protection provisions of Part 5 without a Special Resolution; or
  (c) alters the winding-up right of first offer in clause 32.1 without the Founder's written consent.

### 35. Governing Law

This Constitution is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of **Botswana**. Any dispute arising under this Constitution shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Botswana, except where the parties agree in writing to refer the dispute to arbitration under the Botswana International Arbitration Centre (BIAC) rules.

### 36. Severability

If any provision of this Constitution is held by a competent court to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.
