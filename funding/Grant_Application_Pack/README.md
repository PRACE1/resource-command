# Grant Application Pack — How to Use

Four reusable documents you adapt and submit per grant. None of these are final submissions. They are the **building blocks** every grant application is assembled from.

---

## The four documents

| File | What it is | When you use it |
|---|---|---|
| **01_Executive_Summary.md** | One-page elevator brief | Every grant. The first thing every reviewer reads. |
| **02_Problem_and_Theory_of_Change.md** | Problem framing + ToC framework | Institutional grants (NORAD, AfDB, OSF, EU) require this structure. |
| **03_Budget_Template.md** | Line-item budget with modular sub-budgets per funder | Every grant requires a budget. The sub-budgets per funder are pre-built. |
| **04_Technical_Brief.md** | Two-layer technical specification | Crypto-native grants (PSE, Ethereum Foundation Academic) + EU Horizon technical reviewers |

---

## The order to use them

For any grant application:

1. **Start with the funder's application form.** Read it fully before you write anything.
2. **Identify which of the four documents fits which application section.** Most grants ask for: executive summary → problem statement → theory of change → activities/methodology → budget → team → monitoring framework. The pack covers all of these.
3. **Paste in. Adapt vocabulary.** NORAD uses different language than the Ethereum Foundation. Keep the substance, swap the register.
4. **Sub-budget per funder.** Use the pre-built sub-budget from `03_Budget_Template.md` that matches the funder (PSE, NORAD, AfDB, EU Horizon, OSF). Adjust if the funder's call has a specific amount window.
5. **Attach as supporting documents** where the application allows: ToB SOW (once received), ZEITI engagement letter (once received), demo video, audit pack technical specs.

### Privacy & Scaling Explorations (PSE) — apply first

- **What to lead with:** technical brief (Layer 2). They are cryptographers.
- **Sub-budget to use:** Sub-budget 1, USD 150,000
- **Document priority:** Technical Brief > Executive Summary > Budget. Skip ToC (they don't require it).
- **Special note:** mention Barry Whitehat explicitly. He's part of this ecosystem.

### NORAD — Tax for Development

- **What to lead with:** Problem statement + Theory of Change. They are institutional reviewers.
- **Sub-budget to use:** Sub-budget 2, USD 950,000
- **Document priority:** Executive Summary > Problem & ToC > Budget > Technical Brief (as appendix)
- **Special note:** lead with ZEITI engagement and the Zambian sovereign anchor. De-emphasize the "tech startup" register.

### AfDB

- **What to lead with:** Executive Summary + the AfDB validator-node relationship
- **Sub-budget to use:** Sub-budget 3, USD 500,000
- **Document priority:** Executive Summary > Problem & ToC > Budget. Technical Brief (Layer 1 only).
- **Special note:** position as digital governance / digital public infrastructure, not as a crypto project.

### EU Horizon Europe

- **What to lead with:** Battery Passport regulatory mandate alignment
- **Sub-budget to use:** Sub-budget 4, EUR 1,500,000 (as RC's slice of a consortium)
- **Document priority:** Executive Summary > Technical Brief > Problem & ToC > Budget
- **Special note:** consortium-building is the gating factor. Identify EU partner (Fraunhofer / KU Leuven / TUM type) before writing.

### Open Society Foundations

- **What to lead with:** civil-society alignment, transparency-as-public-good framing
- **Sub-budget to use:** Sub-budget 5, USD 350,000
- **Document priority:** Problem & ToC > Executive Summary > Budget. Technical Brief (Layer 1 only).
- **Special note:** OSF wants to see civil-society partnership (PWYP, NRGI, GW). Lead on the multi-stakeholder governance angle, not the technology.

## What is missing from this pack (deliberately)

The pack does NOT include:

- **Team CVs / org chart.** Will be drafted once first hires are made; founder-only org chart is a weakness for institutional applications.
- **Letters of support.** These come from ZEITI, ZRA, AfDB validators — to be solicited after the 16 June ZEITI call and after the ToB SOW lands.
- **Detailed M&E framework.** Will be drafted to match each grant's M&E specification (DAC, EU Logframe, donor-specific).
- **Risk register beyond the ToC.** Pre-built for the largest applications (NORAD, EU Horizon) at time of submission.
- **Audit pack technical files.** Already exist separately in the audit pack v1.9.

These are deliberate gaps. Each requires either an event to occur first (hire made, letter solicited) or per-grant customisation. Adding placeholder content here would create the impression of false readiness.

## Checklist before submitting any grant

- [ ] Executive Summary updated with current status (ToB engagement signed? ZEITI letter in hand?)
- [ ] Problem & ToC vocabulary matched to funder's house language
- [ ] Sub-budget adjusted to fall inside funder's grant-size window
- [ ] Technical Brief layer matched to reviewer expertise (Layer 1 for institutional, Layer 2 for technical)
- [ ] Supporting documents attached (ToB SOW, ZEITI letter, demo video, audit pack manifest)
- [ ] M&E framework written to match funder's requirement
- [ ] Letters of support solicited from institutional partners
- [ ] Co-funding scenario stated if you are applying to multiple funders in parallel
- [ ] Application reviewed by a non-technical reader before submission (catches jargon residue)
- [ ] Submitted with at least 48 hours of buffer before deadline (no last-minute glitches)
