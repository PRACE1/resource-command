**The structural problem ZCCM-IH carries**

ZCCM-IH holds meaningful stakes across Mopani, CNMC Luanshya, Kariba Minerals, and other operations. The challenge is consistent across all of them: production and royalty declarations flow upward through company reporting systems that a portfolio holder cannot independently verify without audit procedures that are slow, expensive, and contestable. The gap between declared production and independently verifiable production is where compliance risk concentrates – and where royalty disputes originate.

**What Resource Command does**

Resource Command is a zero-knowledge cryptographic compliance system. Mine operators generate a mathematical proof that their royalty calculation is correct – without revealing the underlying production volumes. ZRA, the MRC, and portfolio managers like ZCCM-IH can verify that proof independently and instantly. The declaration becomes tamper-proof at the point of generation, not after a manual audit cycle.

For a portfolio holder managing multiple operations across partners with different systems and incentives, that's a structural assurance layer that doesn't currently exist in the Zambian mining stack.
