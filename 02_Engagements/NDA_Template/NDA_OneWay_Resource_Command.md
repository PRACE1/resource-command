## 1. PURPOSE

The Receiving Party wishes to evaluate a potential business relationship with the Disclosing Party in connection with the Disclosing Party's proprietary system known as **Resource Command** — a zero-knowledge cryptographic protocol for sovereign mineral royalty verification (the "Purpose"). In furtherance of the Purpose, the Disclosing Party may disclose certain Confidential Information (as defined below) to the Receiving Party.

## 2. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

"**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information, whether oral, written, electronic, visual, or in any other form, disclosed by the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party in connection with the Purpose, including without limitation:

(a) the Resource Command system, including its cryptographic circuits, source code, compiled artifacts (`.wasm`, `.r1cs`), constraint design, oracle architecture, and validator consensus design;

(b) any pitch decks, briefs, presentations, technical specifications, audit packs, methodology mappings, or other documents marked or reasonably understood to be confidential;

(c) financial projections, market analysis, business strategy, pricing, capital structure, equity arrangements, and commercial roadmap;

(d) names, titles, and engagement status of institutional counterparties, government contacts, advisors, auditors, validators, investors, and strategic partners;

(e) any product, service, technology, methodology, strategy, or operational plan of the Disclosing Party not in the public domain;

(f) the existence, content, and outcome of discussions between the Parties; and

(g) any analysis, compilation, study, or document prepared by or for the Receiving Party that contains, reflects, or is based upon any of the foregoing.

**Confidential Information does not include** information that: (i) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party; (ii) was rightfully known by the Receiving Party prior to disclosure, evidenced by written records; (iii) is rightfully obtained from a third party without restriction; or (iv) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Confidential Information.

## 3. OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECEIVING PARTY

The Receiving Party shall:

(a) hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and use the same degree of care to protect it as the Receiving Party uses to protect its own confidential information of similar importance, but in no event less than reasonable care;

(b) use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other reason whatsoever;

(c) not disclose, copy, transmit, transfer, or otherwise make available the Confidential Information to any third party (including but not limited to advisors, employees, agents, contractors, or affiliates) without the Disclosing Party's prior written consent, except where such persons have a strict need to know for the Purpose and are themselves bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than those set out herein;

(d) not reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, or otherwise attempt to derive the underlying methodology, source code, algorithms, or constraint structure of any technical artifact disclosed;

(e) not file, register, or attempt to register any patent, trademark, copyright, or other intellectual property right that incorporates, derives from, or is substantially similar to any Confidential Information;

(f) not, during the term of this Agreement and for a period of twenty-four (24) months thereafter, directly or indirectly solicit, engage, or transact with any institutional counterparty, government contact, advisor, auditor, validator, investor, or strategic partner of the Disclosing Party identified or named in the Confidential Information, except where such party is already known to the Receiving Party through a documented prior relationship.

## 4. NO LICENSE OR OWNERSHIP

Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as granting to the Receiving Party any license, ownership, or other right, by implication, estoppel, or otherwise, in or to any Confidential Information or any intellectual property of the Disclosing Party. All Confidential Information remains the sole and exclusive property of the Disclosing Party.

## 5. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION

Upon the Disclosing Party's written request or upon termination of this Agreement, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or, at the Disclosing Party's option, destroy all Confidential Information in its possession, including any copies, excerpts, analyses, or derivative works, and shall certify such return or destruction in writing within seven (7) days.

## 6. TERM

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue in force for a period of **five (5) years** from the Effective Date, except that the non-solicitation obligations in Clause 3(f) shall survive for the period specified therein, and the confidentiality obligations shall survive in respect of any Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret for so long as such information continues to qualify as a trade secret under applicable law.
