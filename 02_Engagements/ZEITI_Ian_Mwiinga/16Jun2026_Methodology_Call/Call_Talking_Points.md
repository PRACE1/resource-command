## Likely questions and the answers to lead with

### Q1: "What is zero-knowledge in plain English?"
**Lead with the audit metaphor.**
> "It is the equivalent of an operator handing ZEITI a sealed envelope. The envelope contains a mathematical certificate that says 'I have calculated my royalty correctly given my actual production figures.' ZEITI can verify the certificate is valid without opening the envelope. The operator's production data never leaves their custody, but the correctness of the royalty calculation becomes mathematically certain rather than procedurally trusted."

### Q2: "How does this not replace the audit function?"
**Lead with what it changes and what it preserves.**
> "It changes one thing: the arithmetic step is no longer something the auditor has to trust. It preserves everything else — the methodology, the audit selection process, ZEITI's reconciliation work, the multi-stakeholder governance. We are not removing humans from the loop; we are removing the part of the loop where humans are guessing whether the operator was honest."

### Q3: "Why would mining operators agree to this?"
**Lead with what they gain, not what they give up.**
> "They keep their commercial confidentiality. Right now, when ZEITI reconciles, the operator's production figures eventually become inferable from aggregate data. With ZK, the figures stay sealed. They also get an EU Battery Passport-compliant attestation as a by-product — the same proof that satisfies ZEITI satisfies the February 2027 EU regulation. They are not adopting a compliance burden; they are getting two compliance regimes for the cost of one."

### Q4: "Who has reviewed this technically?"
**Lead with the institutional ladder.**
> "Trail of Bits — the firm that audited Compound, Aave, the Ethereum Foundation — has the formal audit pack and is in scoping. Independently, Barry Whitehat, who built Semaphore and is one of the foundational figures in ZK identity, has the same pack and has stress-tested the oracle model, the puppet attack vector, the biometric placement, and the temporal dimension. Both reviews are running in parallel."

### Q5: "What is the commercial structure?"
**Lead with what does not change for ZEITI.**
> "ZEITI is not a commercial counterparty. The IP holding entity is in Botswana, licensed to a Zambian operating company that contracts with ZRA. ZEITI's role is methodological — validator, not customer. There is no ask of your institution's budget."

### Q6: "How quickly could a pilot start?"
**Lead with our readiness, not their timeline.**
> "The circuit is built, the audit pack is submitted, the demo is running. From our side, Phase 1 — methodology integration — could start the week after this call. The pacing constraint is ZEITI's Secretariat process, not our build."

### Q7: "What does Phase 3 look like institutionally?"
**Lead with the co-signed methodology note.**
> "The deliverable we would propose is a short ZEITI Secretariat note stating that the cryptographic layer materially strengthens the G-Factor methodology. Not an endorsement of the company — an endorsement of the methodology. That note becomes the institutional anchor for the ZRA conversation that follows."

## Red flags to listen for

- **"This sounds like it would expose mining companies."** → He is hearing it as activist. Re-frame: privacy-preserving, operator-protective.
- **"How does ZEITI stay independent if this is built by a company?"** → He is testing institutional risk. Answer: validator role only, no commercial entanglement, open-source circuit, third-party audited.
- **"The minister is going to want to know about this."** → He is signalling upward escalation. Receive positively. Do not push for the minister introduction on this call — let him offer it.
- **Silence after the audit-envelope metaphor.** → He has understood it and is recalibrating his view. Wait. Do not fill the silence.
