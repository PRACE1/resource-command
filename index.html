Technical Foundation

Satellite Verification (SAR)

Sentinel-1 C-band radar from ESA Copernicus. 10m resolution, 12-day revisit cycle. DEM differencing measures tailings pile volume change independently of any company self-reporting. Accuracy ±8–12%. The 5% audit threshold is set below this band so only statistically significant variances trigger action. The satellite is the smoke detector — the MRC/ZRA joint audit is the fire department.

Source: ESA Copernicus Sentinel-1 · Free public data · No proprietary satellite required

Permissioned Blockchain

Three-node SHA-256 chain. Nodes designated for MRC, ZESCO, and the Auditor General’s Office. The platform vendor holds no node and cannot write to the chain. Two of three must agree before any block is confirmed. Tampering with any historical record breaks every subsequent hash immediately — making silent alteration mathematically impossible.

Architecture: Permissioned BFT consensus · No cryptocurrency · No token · No mining

Zero-Knowledge Proofs

Mining companies prove their royalty calculation is mathematically correct without revealing raw production data to government. The proof is verified on-chain. Government sees "correct" or "incorrect" — never the underlying numbers. This eliminates the political risk of data disputes while maintaining full auditability.

Privacy model: Company data stays on company servers · Only proofs cross the boundary

Legal & Regulatory Basis

MRCA 2024 (Minerals Regulation Commission Act) commenced June 2025. Digital compliance mandatory from January 2027. Section 47(2) requires mandatory joint MRC + ZRA audit within 14 working days for any production variance above 5%. One submission routes simultaneously to all five regulators: MRC, ZEMA, ZRA, ERB, ZCCM-IH.

Status: 44% of operators still on paper · Compliance deadline: January 2027
