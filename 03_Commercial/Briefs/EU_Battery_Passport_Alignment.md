### 2. European Union Critical Raw Materials Act (CRMA)
Under the EU Critical Raw Materials Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1252), Copper is explicitly classified as a **Strategic Raw Material**. The CRMA sets binding supply chain resilience targets, capping dependency on any single third country (e.g., China) at 65% by 2030. 

**The Resource Command Advantage:**
Zambian copper backed by a Resource Command ZK-SNARK proof provides cryptographic provenance, positioning Zambia as a preferred Strategic Partnership candidate for the EU. It enables European manufacturers to frictionlessly prove diversified, responsibly sourced copper procurement under CRMA reporting requirements.

### 3. European Union Battery Regulation (2024–2027) & Cobalt
While Copper falls under the CRMA, **Cobalt** is explicitly mandated under the strict supply chain due diligence requirements of the EU Battery Regulation (Regulation 2023/1542), including the upcoming Digital Battery Passport.

**The Resource Command Advantage:**
Because Zambia produces cobalt as a by-product of copper mining, Resource Command secures both simultaneously. The zero-knowledge proof acts as a cryptographic attestation of compliance without exposing the operator's confidential production margins, granting Zambian cobalt native compliance with the EU Battery Passport mandates.

### 4. United States Inflation Reduction Act (US IRA)
The US IRA (Section 30D) provides massive tax incentives for EV manufacturers that source critical minerals from approved jurisdictions with transparent supply chains. Through the US-led **Minerals Security Partnership (MSP)**, the US is actively seeking non-Chinese supply chains for both copper and cobalt. 

**The Resource Command Advantage:**
By utilizing the World Bank as an independent verification witness on the BFT ledger, Resource Command aligns perfectly with the transparency mandates of the US MSP. Zambian copper and cobalt verified under this protocol positions Zambia as a Tier-1 sourcing partner for North American EV supply chains (e.g., the Lobito Corridor strategy) seeking IRA compliance.
