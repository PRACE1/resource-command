# CONCEPT PAPER: INDEPENDENT MINERAL VERIFICATION INFRASTRUCTURE
## Supporting Revenue Mobilization and Regulatory Integrity under the MRCA 2024
### FOR THE CONSIDERATION OF: THE ZAMBIA REVENUE AUTHORITY (ZRA) & THE MINERALS REGULATION COMMISSION (MRC)

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Zambia has established a world-class mining legal framework through the **Minerals Regulation Commission Act No. 14 of 2024 (MRCA)**. To operationalize the Commission’s mandate for revenue integrity and production oversight, there is a critical need for an independent, technical audit layer that reconciles self-declared operator filings against physical reality. 

**Resource Command** is proposed as the designated technical verification provider for an initial 12-month Regulatory Sandbox. The platform utilizes satellite-derived InSAR telemetry and Zero-Knowledge Proof (ZKP) cryptography to provide independent production audits without compromising operator data sovereignty.

---

## 2. Statutory and Institutional Alignment

### 2.1 The MRCA 2024 Mandate
Section 5 of the MRCA tasks the Commission with the "effective regulation of the mining sector." Resource Command provides the Commission with the independent "Ground Truth" necessary to fulfill this mandate, particularly in the verification of tonnage and mineral content.

### 2.2 ZRA Smart Invoice & MOSES Integration
The platform is designed to integrate seamlessly with the ZRA's **Smart Invoice** and **MOSES** systems. By providing a third-party verification of production at the point of extraction, the platform strengthens the "Revenue Integrity Loop," ensuring that royalty payments are anchored in verifiable physical data.

---

## 3. Technical Architecture: Sovereignty-First

### 3.1 Cryptographic Minimisation (ZKP)
Resource Command utilizes **zk-SNARKs** (Zero-Knowledge Succinct Non-Interactive Arguments of Knowledge). This ensures that sensitive operator data (grades, densities, specific volumes) remains cryptographically hidden, while only "Attestations of Compliance" are shared with the State. This addresses operator concerns regarding commercial confidentiality while meeting the State’s need for verification.

### 3.2 On-Premises Data Sovereignty
In compliance with the **Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021**, all operational data and validator nodes are hosted on-premises within **Infratel Tier 3** facilities in Zambia.

---

## 4. Proposed Sandbox Roadmap
We invite the Commission and the Authority to consider the designation of a **12-month Regulatory Sandbox** to:
1.  Establish technical integration between Resource Command and ZRA/MRC systems.
2.  Calibrate satellite-derived production estimates against ground-truth data from anchor operators (e.g., KoBold Metals Technical Exchange).
3.  Draft implementing regulations under the MRCA 2024 that specify independent verification as a standard for high-value mineral production.

---

## 5. Conclusion
Resource Command represents a "Sovereign Tech Transfer" model. By embedding engineers within the MRC and ZRA teams, we ensure that Zambia remains the master of its own mineral verification layer. We seek a preliminary positioning meeting with the Commissioner General and the MRC Board to refine this institutional alignment.
