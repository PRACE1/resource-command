## Executive Summary
Resource Command is a technical audit layer for Zambia's mineral sector. Integrating ESA Sentinel-1 SAR telemetry, a permissioned blockchain operated by Zambian institutional validators, and Groth16 zero-knowledge proofs, the platform supplies independent, tamper-proof verification that reconciles self-declared operator filings against physical reality. 

The platform is African-domiciled with multi-jurisdictional resilience, delivered through a two-tier structure: **Kgosi Capital Holdings (Botswana) Ltd** (Parent/IP Custodian) and **Kgosi Capital Zambia Ltd** (In-country Operator).

## 3. Economic Leverage and Commercial Framework

### 3.4 Capital Architecture and DTA Positioning
Capitalisation is positioned against the **AfDB FSER-SP (P-ZM-K00-006)**. Financial flows between the Zambian OpCo and Botswana HoldCo are structured under an arm's-length Intellectual Property Licence Agreement, pre-cleared with the **ZRA Transfer Pricing Unit**, and governed by the **Botswana–Zambia Double Taxation Agreement (2013)**. This ensures institutional transparency and tax compliance across all jurisdictions.

---

## 5. Institutional Architecture and Deployment

### 5.3 Data Sovereignty and Cryptographic Minimisation
The primary defense for Zambia's mining data is **Cryptographic Minimisation**. Using Groth16 zk-SNARKs, the platform broadcast only mathematical attestations of compliance. Raw production volumes, grades, and density values never leave the operator's encrypted submission to the MRC validator. 

This technical "moat" is supported by **Section 70 of the Data Protection Act (No. 3 of 2021)**, requiring on-premises hosting within **Infratel-managed Tier 3 data centres** in Zambia. The Botswana parent entity's IP (source code and circuits) is protected under the **Botswana DPA 2021**, providing a complementary layer of regional multi-jurisdictional resilience.

### 5.6 Corporate Architecture
The platform operates a two-tier model:
*   **HoldCo (Botswana):** Registered in Gaborone (host of the SADC Secretariat). Holds the IP (source code, ZKP circuits) and serves as the financing counterparty.
*   **OpCo (Zambia):** Registered at PACRA. Signs the tripartite MOU, employs local engineers, and delivers all in-country services.

This structure allows the project to deliver high **"Local Content"** while maintaining the legal and capital-markets stability required for multilateral investment.
