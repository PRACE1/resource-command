# QUADRIPARTITE SOVEREIGN MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING (MOU)
**Version:** 1.7 (Absolute Final Signature Edition)  
**Date:** 4 May 2026  
**Parties:** 
1.  **The Minerals Regulation Commission (MRC)**, Republic of Zambia  
2.  **The Zambia Revenue Authority (ZRA)**, Republic of Zambia  
3.  **The Zambia Environmental Management Agency (ZEMA)**, Republic of Zambia  
4.  **Kgosi Capital Holdings (Botswana) Ltd**, acting on behalf of **Kgosi Capital Zambia Ltd** (in formation)

---

## 1. Preamble & Objectives
1.1 The Parties recognize the **Resource Command** platform as the primary technical instrument for achieving non-custodial auditability, as specified in **Annex A**.  
1.2 **Amendment Threshold:** Any material change to the Technical Specification in Annex A shall require the consensus of at least **three (3) of the five (5) Validator Nodes** (Simple Majority).

---

## 2. Institutional Framework & Validator Network
2.1 To ensure Byzantine Fault Tolerance (BFT) and sovereign data integrity, the Platform shall be governed by a **Five-Node Validator Network** (n=5, f=1).  
2.2 The Validator Set shall consist of MRC, ZRA, MoF, AfDB, and the World Bank.

---

## 3. Technology Fee & Adjudication Fallback
3.1 In the event of a technical dispute regarding fee calculation, the **AfDB Validator Node** shall act as the primary technical adjudicator.  
3.2 **Fallback Clause:** If the AfDB Node is unable or unwilling to adjudicate within ten (10) business days, the **World Bank Validator Node** shall automatically assume the role of technical adjudicator.

---

## 4. Legal Status of Kgosi Capital
4.1 This MOU is executed by **Kgosi Capital Holdings (Botswana) Ltd**.  
4.2 Kgosi Capital Holdings hereby undertakes to complete the registration of **Kgosi Capital Zambia Ltd** (the "OpCo") as a condition precedent to the commencement of the Sandbox Phase 2. All local operational mandates shall be transferred to the OpCo upon successful PACRA registration.

---

## 5. Dispute Resolution & Governing Law
5.1 This MOU and any dispute or claim arising out of it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the **Laws of the Republic of Zambia**.  
5.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this MOU shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration under the **LCIA Rules**. The seat of arbitration shall be **Mauritius**.
