## SECTION 1 — PRELIMINARY AND INTERPRETATION

### 1.1 Definitions

In this Constitution, unless the context otherwise requires:

- **"Act"** means the Companies Act (Cap. 42:01) of Botswana, including all regulations and amendments thereto, and specifically including the Companies (Amendment) Act, 2025;
- **"Board"** means the board of directors of the Company as constituted from time to time;
- **"Business Day"** means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday recognised in Botswana;
- **"CIPA"** means the Companies and Intellectual Property Authority of Botswana established under the Companies Act;
- **"Company"** means Kgosi Sovereign Holdings (Proprietary) Limited;
- **"Company Secretary"** means the person appointed as company secretary of the Company from time to time;
- **"Constitution"** means this Company Constitution as amended from time to time in accordance with Section 25;
- **"Director"** means a person appointed as a director of the Company in accordance with this Constitution;
- **"Financial Year"** means the financial year of the Company as determined by the Board, being the twelve-month period ending on 31 December in each year, unless otherwise changed by the Board by resolution;
- **"Good Leaver"** means a Shareholder who ceases to be an employee or director of the Company by reason of death, permanent disability, retirement with Board consent, or mutual written agreement with the Board;
- **"Bad Leaver"** means any Shareholder who ceases to be an employee or director of the Company in circumstances other than those constituting a Good Leaver;
- **"Intellectual Property"** or **"IP"** has the meaning given in Section 18.1;
- **"Ordinary Resolution"** means a resolution approved by a simple majority (more than 50%) of the votes of those Shareholders entitled to vote and voting in person or by proxy;
- **"Ordinary Share"** means a share in the capital of the Company classified as an Ordinary Share under Section 5;
- **"Platform"** means the Resource Command protocol, zero-knowledge proof compliance engine, and all related software, algorithms, data structures, APIs, circuits, and infrastructure developed or owned by the Company;
- **"Registrar"** means the Registrar of Companies at CIPA;
- **"Reserved Matter"** means any matter listed in Section 10 of this Constitution;
- **"Share"** means a share in the stated capital of the Company;
- **"Shareholder"** means a person registered in the Company's register of members as a holder of Shares;
- **"Solvency Test"** means the test set out in the Act which the Board must satisfy before making any distribution, approving a share buyback, or providing financial assistance, namely that: (a) the Company's assets fairly valued equal or exceed its liabilities fairly valued; and (b) the Company will be able to pay its debts as they fall due in the normal course of business;
- **"Special Resolution"** means a resolution approved by not less than seventy-five percent (75%) of the votes of those Shareholders entitled to vote and voting in person or by proxy, with at least twenty-one (21) days' prior written notice having been given;
- **"Sovereign Entity"** means any government, government ministry or department, government agency, sovereign wealth fund, multilateral development bank, international organisation, or regulatory authority;
- **"Written Resolution"** means a resolution of the Board or Shareholders passed in accordance with Section 12.5 without the need for a formal meeting.

### 1.2 Interpretation

In this Constitution, unless the context otherwise requires:

(a) words in the singular include the plural, and vice versa;  
(b) a reference to a person includes a company, partnership, trust, unincorporated association and any other entity;  
(c) a reference to a Section is to a section of this Constitution;  
(d) headings are for convenience only and shall not affect interpretation;  
(e) a reference to the Act includes any regulations made thereunder and any amendment or re-enactment thereof;  
(f) where the Act requires anything to be done or prohibited and this Constitution is silent, the Act governs.

## SECTION 5 — SHARE CAPITAL AND RIGHTS

### 5.1 Authorised Share Capital

The authorised share capital of the Company shall consist of:

(a) **10,000 (ten thousand) Class A Ordinary Shares** of no par value;  
(b) **40,000 (forty thousand) Class B Ordinary Shares** of no par value;  
(c) **50,000 (fifty thousand) Class C Preference Shares** of no par value.

The Board may, by Special Resolution of Shareholders, increase or vary the authorised share capital.

### 5.2 Class A Ordinary Shares — Rights

Each Class A Ordinary Share shall carry the following rights:

(a) **Voting Rights**: Ten (10) votes per share on all resolutions put to a general meeting of Shareholders (weighted voting — founder protection);  
(b) **Dividend Rights**: Equal right to participate in any dividends declared by the Board on a per-share basis, ranking pari passu with Class B Ordinary Shares, after any preferential dividend on Class C Preference Shares has been paid;  
(c) **Winding Up**: Equal right to participate in the distribution of surplus assets upon winding up, ranking pari passu with Class B Ordinary Shares, after all debts and after Class C liquidation preferences have been satisfied;  
(d) **Conversion**: Class A Ordinary Shares shall automatically convert to Class B Ordinary Shares (on a one-for-one basis) upon a transfer to any person who is not a Permitted Transferee of the original holder, unless the Board resolves otherwise by unanimous board resolution.

### 5.3 Class B Ordinary Shares — Rights

Each Class B Ordinary Share shall carry the following rights:

(a) **Voting Rights**: One (1) vote per share on all resolutions put to a general meeting of Shareholders;  
(b) **Dividend Rights**: Equal right to participate in any dividends declared by the Board on a per-share basis, ranking pari passu with Class A Ordinary Shares, after any preferential dividend on Class C Preference Shares has been paid;  
(c) **Winding Up**: Equal right to participate in the distribution of surplus assets upon winding up, ranking pari passu with Class A Ordinary Shares, after all debts and after Class C liquidation preferences have been satisfied.

### 5.4 Class C Preference Shares — Rights

Each Class C Preference Share shall carry the following rights:

(a) **Voting Rights**: No voting rights save in respect of resolutions which directly vary, alter, or abrogate the rights attaching to Class C Preference Shares, in which case each Class C Preference Share shall carry one (1) vote;  
(b) **Preferential Dividend**: A preferential dividend at a fixed cumulative rate of eight percent (8%) per annum on the issue price of such shares, calculated from the date of issue. Such dividends are cumulative and shall accrue whether or not profits are available. No dividend shall be paid on Class A or Class B Ordinary Shares until all accrued and unpaid preferential dividends on the Class C Preference Shares have been paid in full;  
(c) **Winding Up / Liquidation Preference**: On a winding up or liquidation, Class C Preference Shareholders shall be entitled to receive, in priority to all other Shareholders, an amount equal to the original issue price of their shares plus all accrued but unpaid preferential dividends before any distribution is made to ordinary Shareholders;  
(d) **Conversion**: Class C Preference Shares are convertible to Class B Ordinary Shares at the option of the holder, at a conversion ratio to be agreed at the time of issue and recorded in the relevant subscription agreement;  
(e) **Redemption**: Class C Preference Shares are redeemable at the option of the Company, subject to the Solvency Test being satisfied and the Board passing a resolution approving such redemption.

### 5.5 Modification of Class Rights

The rights attaching to any class of Shares may only be varied, altered, or abrogated by:

(a) a Special Resolution of the general meeting of the Company; AND  
(b) either (i) the written consent of not less than three-quarters (75%) of the holders of the issued shares of that class, or (ii) a Special Resolution passed at a separate class meeting of the holders of that class.

## SECTION 6 — TRANSFER OF SHARES

### 6.1 Restriction on Transfer

No Share shall be transferred except in compliance with this Section 6. Any purported transfer in breach of this Section shall be void and of no effect.

### 6.2 Pre-Emption Right on Transfer (Right of First Refusal)

(a) Any Shareholder wishing to transfer all or any of their Shares (the **"Transferring Shareholder"**) must first give written notice (a **"Transfer Notice"**) to the Board specifying: (i) the number and class of Shares proposed to be transferred; (ii) the identity of the proposed transferee; (iii) the proposed transfer price; and (iv) the material terms of the proposed transfer.

(b) The Board shall, within fifteen (15) Business Days of receiving the Transfer Notice, offer the available Shares to the remaining Shareholders (other than the Transferring Shareholder) pro rata to their existing shareholdings (the **"Pre-emption Offer"**).

(c) Remaining Shareholders shall have twenty (20) Business Days to accept the Pre-emption Offer in writing (the **"Acceptance Period"**). Any Shareholder who does not accept within the Acceptance Period shall be deemed to have waived their pre-emption right in respect of that transfer.

(d) If the remaining Shareholders do not take up all the available Shares, the Transferring Shareholder may, within sixty (60) Business Days of the expiry of the Acceptance Period, transfer the remaining unsold Shares to the proposed transferee named in the Transfer Notice at no less than the price stated in the Transfer Notice, subject to Board approval under Section 6.3.

(e) If the transfer to the proposed third-party transferee is not completed within the sixty (60) Business Day period, the pre-emption procedure must be repeated.

### 6.3 Board Approval of Transfers

No transfer of Shares shall be registered unless approved by the Board by ordinary resolution. The Board may, in its absolute discretion, decline to approve the registration of any transfer to a person they deem not to be in the best interests of the Company. The Board must communicate its decision within thirty (30) Business Days of receiving the request; failure to do so within this period shall be deemed approval.

### 6.4 Permitted Transfers

Notwithstanding Section 6.2 and 6.3, a Shareholder may transfer Shares without triggering the pre-emption procedure to the following **"Permitted Transferees"**, subject to prior written notice to the Board:

(a) A wholly-owned subsidiary or holding company of the Transferring Shareholder;  
(b) A family trust of which the Transferring Shareholder or their immediate family members are the primary beneficiaries;  
(c) The spouse or lineal descendants of the Transferring Shareholder (for estate planning purposes);  

provided that if the Permitted Transferee ceases to be a Permitted Transferee, the Shares shall immediately be transferred back to the original Shareholder or to another Permitted Transferee. A transfer to a Permitted Transferee shall cause any Class A Ordinary Shares to remain Class A (unlike transfers to third parties under Section 5.2(d)).

### 6.5 Drag-Along Rights

(a) If Shareholders holding not less than seventy-five percent (75%) of all issued and voting Shares (the **"Dragging Shareholders"**) resolve to sell all of their Shares to a bona fide third-party acquirer (the **"Acquirer"**) in a single transaction or series of related transactions, then the Dragging Shareholders may serve written notice on the remaining Shareholders (the **"Drag Notice"**) requiring them to sell all (but not some) of their Shares to the Acquirer on the same terms and at the same price per share as received by the Dragging Shareholders.

(b) No drag-along may be effected at a price below the fair market value of the Shares as independently determined or agreed between the parties.

(c) The Drag Notice must be served not less than twenty (20) Business Days prior to the proposed completion date.

### 6.6 Tag-Along Rights

(a) If a Shareholder or group of Shareholders (the **"Selling Shareholders"**) propose to sell Shares amounting to thirty percent (30%) or more of all issued Shares to a third party, the remaining Shareholders (the **"Tag-Along Holders"**) shall have the right (but not the obligation) to require the Selling Shareholders to procure that the third-party purchaser acquires all of their Shares on the same terms and at the same price per share as the Selling Shareholders.

(b) The Selling Shareholders must serve written notice on all Tag-Along Holders at least twenty (20) Business Days before completion of the proposed sale, setting out the material terms of the proposed sale.

(c) Tag-Along Holders wishing to exercise their tag-along rights must give written notice to the Selling Shareholders within ten (10) Business Days of receipt of the tag-along notice.

### 6.7 Leaver Provisions

(a) Upon a Shareholder who is also an employee or director of the Company ceasing to be so (a **"Leaver"**), the Company shall have the option (but not the obligation) to purchase the Leaver's Shares at a price to be determined as follows:

  (i) **Good Leaver**: Fair market value of the Shares as at the date of cessation, as agreed between the parties or, failing agreement, as determined by the Company's auditors acting as experts;  
  (ii) **Bad Leaver**: The lower of fair market value or the original subscription price paid by the Leaver.

(b) The Company must exercise its option under Section 6.7(a) within sixty (60) Business Days of the relevant cessation date by written notice to the Leaver.

(c) If the Company does not exercise its option, the pre-emption provisions of Section 6.2 shall apply.

## SECTION 7 — ISSUE OF NEW SHARES

### 7.1 Board Authority

Subject to Section 7.2 and any applicable Reserved Matters, the Board is authorised to issue Shares within the authorised share capital set out in Section 5.1 on such terms as the Board may determine.

### 7.2 Pre-Emption on New Issuances

(a) Before issuing any new Shares (other than in a Reserved Matter transaction requiring Shareholder approval), the Board shall first offer such new Shares to existing Shareholders pro rata to their existing shareholdings, at the proposed issue price, with at least fifteen (15) Business Days to accept.

(b) Pre-emption rights under this Section 7.2 may be dis-applied by a Special Resolution of Shareholders in respect of a specific issuance (for example, to facilitate a third-party investor round).

### 7.3 Terms of Issue

All Shares must be fully paid on issuance. No Shares shall be issued at a discount to their fair market value except as approved by Special Resolution.

## SECTION 8 — DIRECTORS

### 8.1 Number of Directors

The Board shall consist of a minimum of one (1) and a maximum of seven (7) Directors.

### 8.2 Appointment of Directors

(a) The initial Director(s) of the Company shall be appointed by the founder upon incorporation.  
(b) The Board may appoint additional Directors by Ordinary Resolution of the Board at any time, subject to the maximum number in Section 8.1.  
(c) Shareholders may appoint Directors by Ordinary Resolution at a general meeting.  
(d) Shareholders holding not less than fifty-one percent (51%) of all issued voting Shares shall at all times have the right to appoint and remove Directors by Ordinary Resolution.

### 8.3 Removal of Directors

A Director may be removed from office at any time by Ordinary Resolution of the Shareholders.

### 8.4 Vacation of Office

A Director shall cease to hold office if the Director:

(a) resigns by written notice to the Company;  
(b) is disqualified from acting as a director under the Act;  
(c) is declared insolvent or makes any arrangement or composition with creditors generally;  
(d) is of unsound mind or incapable of performing their duties;  
(e) is convicted of any offence involving dishonesty, fraud, or moral turpitude;  
(f) ceases to hold any share qualification (if any is required by the Board); or  
(g) is removed by resolution of Shareholders under Section 8.3.

### 8.5 Powers of the Board

(a) Subject to this Constitution and the Act, the Board shall have full authority to manage the business and affairs of the Company and may exercise all powers of the Company that are not, by this Constitution or the Act, required to be exercised by the Shareholders.

(b) Without limiting Section 8.5(a), the Board shall have authority to:

  (i) carry on the business and operations of the Company;  
  (ii) borrow and raise money for the purposes of the Company and grant mortgages, charges, pledges, and other security over the assets of the Company;  
  (iii) enter into any agreement, contract, or commitment on behalf of the Company;  
  (iv) appoint and remove officers, employees, and consultants and determine their remuneration;  
  (v) issue Shares within the authorised share capital (subject to Section 7);  
  (vi) open bank accounts and operate them;  
  (vii) invest the Company's funds in accordance with a treasury policy approved by the Board;  
  (viii) approve capital expenditure and operating expenditure within approved budgets;  
  (ix) delegate any of its powers to a committee or officer of the Company (except powers which the Act requires to be exercised by the Board itself).

### 8.6 Director Duties

Each Director shall, in performing their functions, comply with the director's duties as set out in the Act, which include:

(a) acting in good faith and in the best interests of the Company;  
(b) exercising powers for proper purposes;  
(c) exercising reasonable care, diligence, and skill;  
(d) avoiding conflicts of interest and disclosing any personal interest in any Company matter;  
(e) not using Company information, assets, or opportunities for personal benefit without Board consent;  
(f) not trading recklessly or knowingly incurring obligations the Company cannot meet.

### 8.7 Conflict of Interest

(a) A Director who has a personal interest (direct or indirect) in any transaction, agreement, or decision being considered by the Board shall forthwith disclose such interest to the Board.  
(b) Such disclosure shall be recorded in the minutes of the Board meeting.  
(c) A Director with a disclosed conflict of interest shall not vote on the relevant matter and shall not be counted towards the quorum for that matter.  
(d) If all Directors have a conflict of interest in a matter, the matter shall be referred to the Shareholders for approval.

### 8.8 Director Remuneration

Directors shall not be entitled to any remuneration by virtue of their office unless such remuneration is approved by the Shareholders by Ordinary Resolution. The Board may, however, approve the reimbursement of reasonable expenses incurred by Directors in performance of their duties.

### 8.9 Indemnification of Directors

The Company shall indemnify each Director against all costs, charges, losses, expenses, and liabilities incurred by the Director in the execution and discharge of their duties or in connection with any claim relating to anything done or omitted in the course of their duties, to the maximum extent permitted by the Act.

### 8.10 Chairperson

The Board shall elect a Chairperson from among the Directors. The Chairperson shall preside at all Board meetings and general meetings. In the event of an equality of votes at a Board meeting, the Chairperson shall have a second or casting vote.

## SECTION 9 — BOARD MEETINGS

### 9.1 Frequency

The Board shall meet as often as necessary for the proper conduct of business, but not less than four (4) times per Financial Year.

### 9.2 Notice

(a) Each Director shall be given at least five (5) Business Days' notice of a Board meeting, specifying the date, time, venue, and agenda.  
(b) Notice may be given by email, instant message platform, or any other electronic means agreed by the Board.  
(c) A Director may waive notice of any Board meeting, either before or after the meeting.

### 9.3 Quorum

(a) The quorum for a Board meeting shall be a majority of the Directors then in office, subject to a minimum of one (1) Director.  
(b) If a quorum is not present within thirty (30) minutes of the scheduled start time, the meeting shall be adjourned to the same time and place on the next Business Day.

### 9.4 Voting

(a) Each Director shall have one (1) vote on all matters put to a Board meeting.  
(b) Decisions of the Board shall be made by a simple majority of votes cast by Directors present and voting.  
(c) Except where a Reserved Matter requires a higher threshold, no supermajority is required for Board resolutions.

### 9.5 Meetings by Electronic Means

A Board meeting may be held by video conference, telephone conference, or other electronic means provided all participating Directors can simultaneously hear each other. Directors so participating shall be deemed to be present in person.

### 9.6 Written Resolutions of the Board

A resolution of the Board may be passed without a meeting if a written resolution is signed (including electronically) by all Directors entitled to vote on the resolution. Such resolution shall be as valid as if passed at a duly convened Board meeting.

## SECTION 10 — RESERVED MATTERS

### 10.1 Shareholder Reserved Matters

The following matters shall require a **Special Resolution** of Shareholders (75% of votes) and may not be authorised by the Board alone:

(a) amendment of this Constitution;  
(b) change of the Company's name;  
(c) creation of a new class of Shares or modification of rights attaching to any existing class of Shares;  
(d) increase of authorised share capital;  
(e) any issue of Shares where pre-emption rights are to be dis-applied;  
(f) sale, disposal, or transfer of all or substantially all of the Company's assets or business;  
(g) any merger, amalgamation, acquisition of another company, or restructuring;  
(h) voluntary winding up or dissolution of the Company;  
(i) any change to the Company's Financial Year;  
(j) approval of dividends or other distributions (the Board may resolve to declare dividends subject to the Solvency Test, but any dividend above a threshold equivalent to fifty percent (50%) of the Company's after-tax profits requires a Special Resolution);  
(k) any transaction between the Company and a Director or a Director's connected person (related party transaction), unless on arms-length commercial terms approved by the independent Directors;  
(l) any grant of security, mortgage, or charge over all or substantially all of the Company's assets.

### 10.2 Board Reserved Matters (Supermajority of Board)

The following matters shall require the approval of not less than seventy-five percent (75%) of all Directors then in office (not merely those present at a meeting):

(a) entry into any contract with a Sovereign Entity valued at or above BWP 1,000,000 (one million Pula) or the US Dollar equivalent;  
(b) entry into any licensing agreement for the Platform or any material Intellectual Property with a third party;  
(c) commencement or settlement of any legal or arbitral proceedings involving the Company;  
(d) any borrowing exceeding BWP 500,000 (five hundred thousand Pula) or the equivalent;  
(e) approval of the annual budget and business plan;  
(f) appointment or removal of the Company's auditors;  
(g) establishment of any subsidiary, branch, joint venture, or representative office;  
(h) any capital expenditure not included in an approved budget exceeding BWP 100,000 (one hundred thousand Pula);  
(i) any transaction which in the reasonable opinion of any Director may involve the Company's Intellectual Property in a manner not contemplated by existing agreements.

## SECTION 11 — SHAREHOLDERS AND GENERAL MEETINGS

### 11.1 Annual General Meeting

(a) The Board shall call an Annual General Meeting (AGM) of Shareholders within six (6) months of the end of each Financial Year.  
(b) The AGM shall transact such business as is required by the Act, including the consideration of annual financial statements, election of Directors (if required), and appointment of auditors (if applicable).

### 11.2 Special General Meetings

(a) The Board may at any time convene a Special General Meeting of Shareholders.  
(b) Shareholders holding not less than twenty percent (20%) of all issued voting Shares may by written requisition require the Board to convene a Special General Meeting, specifying the business to be transacted. The Board must convene such meeting within twenty-one (21) days of receiving such requisition.

### 11.3 Notice of Meetings

(a) At least twenty-one (21) days' written notice of a general meeting shall be given to all Shareholders and Directors, specifying the date, time, venue, and business to be transacted.  
(b) Notice of meetings may be given by email, WhatsApp, or other electronic means.  
(c) A shorter period of notice shall be valid if consented to in writing by all Shareholders.

### 11.4 Quorum

The quorum for a general meeting shall be at least one (1) Shareholder present in person or by proxy, representing not less than fifty-one percent (51%) of all issued voting Shares. If a quorum is not present within thirty (30) minutes, the meeting shall be adjourned to the same time and place seven (7) days later, at which adjourned meeting the Shareholders present shall constitute a quorum.

### 11.5 Proxies

A Shareholder may appoint any person as proxy to attend, speak, and vote on their behalf at any general meeting. A proxy need not be a Shareholder. Proxy forms must be lodged at the registered office or by email not less than forty-eight (48) hours before the meeting.

### 11.6 Electronic Meetings

General meetings may be held by video conference or other electronic means provided all Shareholders can simultaneously see and hear each other. Shareholders so participating shall be deemed to be present in person.

### 11.7 Voting at Meetings

(a) Every resolution put to a general meeting shall be decided in the first instance on a show of hands (or by electronic count), unless a poll is demanded.  
(b) On a poll, every Shareholder present or by proxy shall have the number of votes corresponding to the number and class of Shares they hold (taking into account weighted voting rights for Class A Ordinary Shares under Section 5.2(a)).  
(c) In the case of an equality of votes, the Chairperson shall have a second or casting vote.

## SECTION 12 — RESOLUTIONS

### 12.1 Ordinary Resolutions

An Ordinary Resolution is passed by a simple majority (more than 50%) of the votes of Shareholders present and voting at a duly convened general meeting.

### 12.2 Special Resolutions

A Special Resolution is passed by not less than seventy-five percent (75%) of the votes of Shareholders present and voting at a general meeting of which not less than twenty-one (21) days' written notice has been given specifying the intention to propose the resolution as a Special Resolution.

### 12.3 Board Resolutions

Board resolutions are passed by a simple majority of votes of Directors present and voting at a duly constituted Board meeting, subject to Section 10.2 requirements.

### 12.4 Written Resolutions of Shareholders

Any resolution (whether Ordinary or Special) may be passed without a meeting if all Shareholders entitled to vote on the resolution sign a written resolution to that effect. Such resolution shall be as valid and effective as if passed at a duly convened general meeting.

### 12.5 Filing Requirements

Special Resolutions shall be filed with CIPA within the time period required by the Act after being passed.

## SECTION 13 — DISTRIBUTIONS (DIVIDENDS)

### 13.1 Declaration of Dividends

(a) The Board may, by resolution, declare dividends on any class of Shares provided that the Solvency Test is satisfied immediately before and immediately after the proposed distribution.  
(b) Before declaring any dividend, the Board must satisfy itself that the Solvency Test is met and must record such satisfaction in the relevant Board resolution.

### 13.2 Priority of Dividends

Dividends shall be paid in the following order of priority:

(a) First, to Class C Preference Shareholders, the preferential cumulative dividend at the rate specified in Section 5.4(b), in respect of all accrued and unpaid amounts;  
(b) Thereafter, to Class A and Class B Ordinary Shareholders, pari passu, in proportion to the number of Ordinary Shares held.

### 13.3 No Obligation to Declare

Nothing in this Constitution obliges the Board to declare any dividend. The Board has full discretion as to the amount and timing of any dividend.

### 13.4 Dividend in Kind

The Board may declare a dividend payable in assets other than cash, provided that all Shareholders of the relevant class receive equivalent value.

## SECTION 14 — SHARE BUYBACKS AND FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE

### 14.1 Share Buybacks

The Company may purchase or repurchase its own Shares provided that:

(a) the Solvency Test is satisfied immediately before and after the buyback;  
(b) a Board resolution approving the buyback is passed;  
(c) if the aggregate of all buybacks in any Financial Year exceeds ten percent (10%) of the Company's stated capital, an auditor's certificate confirming solvency is obtained.

### 14.2 Financial Assistance

The Company shall not provide financial assistance (including loans, guarantees, or security) for the purpose of or in connection with the acquisition of its own Shares unless:

(a) the Solvency Test is satisfied;  
(b) the Board passes a resolution approving the financial assistance; and  
(c) where required by the Act, an auditor's certificate is obtained.

The Board shall appoint a Company Secretary and shall at all times maintain a Company Secretary as required by the Act. The Company Secretary may be a Director of the Company if permitted by the Act.

The Company Secretary may be removed by the Board at any time by ordinary Board resolution.

## SECTION 18 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

### 18.1 Definition of Intellectual Property

**"Intellectual Property"** or **"IP"** means all rights in and to:

(a) all software code, source code, object code, algorithms, scripts, programmes, and applications;  
(b) all zero-knowledge proof circuits, constraint systems (R1CS), proving keys, verification keys, and associated cryptographic artefacts;  
(c) all databases, data structures, APIs, and data compilations;  
(d) all protocols, architectures, system designs, technical specifications, and documentation;  
(e) all platform designs, user interfaces, and user experience designs;  
(f) all trade secrets, know-how, and proprietary methodologies;  
(g) all trade marks, service marks, brand names, logos, and domain names;  
(h) all copyright works, whether or not registered;  
(i) all applications for and rights to apply for any of the foregoing;  
(j) all derivatives, modifications, adaptations, or improvements of any of the foregoing;  
in each case whether created, developed, or acquired by or on behalf of the Company.

### 18.2 Company Ownership

All Intellectual Property created, invented, developed, or discovered by any Director, employee, officer, contractor, or consultant of the Company in the course of or in connection with their engagement with the Company shall immediately vest in and be owned exclusively by the Company.

### 18.3 IP Assignment

(a) Every Director, employee, officer, contractor, and consultant of the Company shall execute an IP assignment agreement in the Company's standard form upon commencement of their engagement.  
(b) Such agreement shall confirm assignment to the Company of all present and future IP developed in connection with the Company's business.  
(c) No Director, officer, employee, contractor, or consultant shall retain any personal rights in Company IP after their engagement ends.

### 18.4 No Unauthorised Licensing or Disclosure

No Director or officer of the Company shall license, sublicense, disclose, transfer, or otherwise deal with any Company IP to or with any third party without express Board authorisation. Any licensing of the Platform, the circuits, or any core technology to a third party shall be a Board Reserved Matter under Section 10.2.

### 18.5 Open-Source Policy

No Company IP shall be released under any open-source licence without prior approval of the Board by a resolution of not less than seventy-five percent (75%) of all Directors.

### 18.6 Trademark Registration

The Board shall take all reasonable steps to register the Company's trade marks and brand assets with CIPA and in relevant foreign jurisdictions to protect the Company's commercial identity.

## SECTION 19 — CONFIDENTIALITY

### 19.1 General Confidentiality Obligations

Each Director and Shareholder of the Company shall keep strictly confidential and shall not disclose to any third party (without prior written Board approval):

(a) the technical design, architecture, and source code of the Platform or any Company IP;  
(b) the financial affairs and commercial terms of any Company contract;  
(c) the terms of any engagement with any Sovereign Entity;  
(d) any information relating to any investor, customer, or counterparty of the Company;  
(e) any information designated as confidential by the Board;  
(f) any information provided under any Non-Disclosure Agreement to which the Company is a party.

### 19.2 Permitted Disclosures

The confidentiality obligations in Section 19.1 shall not apply to:

(a) information which is or becomes public knowledge through no fault of the disclosing party;  
(b) disclosures required by law, court order, or regulatory authority (in which case the disclosing party shall, to the extent permitted by law, give the Company as much prior written notice as possible);  
(c) disclosures made with the prior written approval of the Board.

### 19.3 Duration

The obligations in this Section 19 shall continue during the period a person is a Director or Shareholder and for a period of three (3) years following cessation of their directorship or disposal of all their Shares.

## SECTION 20 — GOVERNMENT AND SOVEREIGN CONTRACTS

### 20.1 Board Approval

The Company shall not enter into any contract, agreement, memorandum of understanding, or arrangement with any Sovereign Entity involving a value or commitment of BWP 1,000,000 (one million Pula) or more (or the equivalent in any foreign currency) without the prior approval of not less than seventy-five percent (75%) of all Directors.

### 20.2 Anti-Bribery and Corruption

(a) All Directors, officers, employees, and agents of the Company shall comply with the Corruption and Economic Crime Act (Cap. 08:05) of Botswana and all applicable anti-corruption laws in jurisdictions where the Company operates.  
(b) No Director, officer, employee, or agent shall offer, pay, give, or authorise any bribe, facilitation payment, or other improper payment to any government official, public servant, or employee of a Sovereign Entity.  
(c) Any Director who becomes aware of any actual or suspected corruption shall immediately report it to the Board and, if required by law, to the Directorate on Corruption and Economic Crime.

### 20.3 Know Your Client Obligations

The Company shall conduct appropriate due diligence on all Sovereign Entity counterparties and shall maintain records of such due diligence in compliance with the Financial Intelligence Act of Botswana and applicable anti-money laundering regulations.

### 20.4 Confidentiality of Government Contracts

All contracts with Sovereign Entities and all related tender, procurement, and negotiation information shall be treated as confidential information under Section 19 and shall not be disclosed to any third party without the prior written approval of the Board and, where required, the relevant Sovereign Entity counterparty.

### 22.2 Data Minimisation

## SECTION 23 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION

### 23.1 Internal Escalation

Any dispute between Shareholders, or between a Shareholder and the Company, in connection with this Constitution shall first be referred to the Board for resolution within twenty (20) Business Days of written notice of the dispute being given to all Directors.

### 23.2 Mediation

If a dispute is not resolved through the Board escalation process under Section 23.1, the parties shall attempt to resolve it through mediation before a mutually agreed mediator within thirty (30) Business Days.

### 23.3 Arbitration

(a) If a dispute is not resolved through mediation within the period specified in Section 23.2, either party may refer the dispute to binding arbitration in accordance with the rules of the Botswana Arbitration Act (Cap. 06:01) or, if the parties agree, the rules of the African Arbitration Association or such other international arbitration institution as the parties may agree.

(b) The arbitration shall be conducted:  
  (i) in the English language;  
  (ii) by a sole arbitrator agreed between the parties or, failing agreement within ten (10) Business Days, appointed by the chairperson of the Law Society of Botswana;  
  (iii) at a venue in Gaborone, Botswana, unless the parties agree otherwise.

(c) The award of the arbitrator shall be final and binding on the parties.

### 23.4 Governing Law

This Constitution and any dispute arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of Botswana.

## SECTION 24 — WINDING UP

### 24.1 Voluntary Winding Up

The Company may be wound up voluntarily by a Special Resolution of Shareholders. No voluntary winding up shall be approved if the Company is unable to pay its debts.

### 24.2 Distribution of Assets

On a winding up, the assets of the Company available for distribution after payment of all debts and liabilities shall be distributed in the following order:

(a) First, to Class C Preference Shareholders: the original issue price of their shares plus all accrued but unpaid preferential dividends;  
(b) Thereafter, to Class A and Class B Ordinary Shareholders pro rata to the number of Ordinary Shares held.

### 24.3 Distribution in Kind

If assets are not readily realisable, the liquidator may, with the approval of a Special Resolution of Shareholders, distribute assets in kind in satisfaction of Shareholders' entitlements.
