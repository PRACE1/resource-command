Section 1

What Problem Are We Solving?

Imagine a gold mine that digs up a mountain of copper — but tells the government it only dug up a small hill. The government has no easy way to check. That missing money is called royalty leakage.

Simple Version: Mining companies pay a fee to the government for every tonne of copper they dig up. This fee is called a "royalty." Some companies secretly under-report how much they dug — so they pay less than they should. Resource Command is a tool that catches this — without spying on the company's private business secrets.
❌
The Old Problem
  • Government can't see inside the mine
  • Companies report their own numbers
  • No independent way to verify
  • Billions lost every year in unpaid royalties
  • Arguments end up in court for years
✅
Our Solution
  • Satellites watch the mine from space
  • Math (not humans) checks the numbers
  • Company secrets stay private
  • Government gets a "Proof" — like a receipt
  • Disputes are resolved in minutes, not years

The Secret Ingredient

Zero-Knowledge Proofs — Like a Magic Envelope

Imagine you put the answer to a maths problem in a sealed envelope. You can prove you know the answer — without opening the envelope. That's what a Zero-Knowledge Proof does. The mining company proves they paid the right royalty without showing their private production numbers.

🏭
The Mining Company (Prover)
Knows the secret numbers: volume, density, grade. Keeps them private.

What the Government Receives

A cryptographic receipt that says: "This company paid the correct 6% royalty on their production. We cannot tell you how much they produced — but we can guarantee the maths is right."

Section 6

The Legal Framework

A great technology with weak contracts is like a bulletproof door with no hinges. Here's what we built on the legal side.

📄

Quadripartite MOU v1.7

A four-party agreement between MRC, ZRA, ZEMA, and Kgosi Capital. Governs the 12-month Regulatory Sandbox. Legally locked to the Technical Spec via a SHA-256 fingerprint — if anyone edits the spec, the fingerprint changes and the mismatch is visible.

⚖️

LCIA Arbitration · Mauritius Seat

Any disputes go to the London Court of International Arbitration with Mauritius as the neutral seat. This is the gold standard for African sovereign commercial contracts. No home-court advantage.

🏢

PACRA Condition Precedent

The contract cannot fully activate until Kgosi Capital Zambia Ltd is officially registered locally. This protects the government — they're not signing with a ghost company.

🔐

Version-Locked Technical Spec

Annex A in the MOU contains the SHA-256 hash of the exact Technical Specification document. Any future change requires 3-of-5 validator consensus to approve.
